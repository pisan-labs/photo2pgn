Refund policy

Last updated: Draft for review, 13 November 2026

Credits for Photo to PGN are sold by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record, on behalf of HintWise.

Within 14 days

  • A pack none of whose credits you have used: a full refund.
  • A pack you have partly used: we refund the unused credits, at the price per credit you paid.

After 14 days

Unused credits do not expire, so you can keep using them. We refund them later only where the law requires it, or at our discretion.

Readings that went wrong

A reading that does not go through (an error from Anthropic or from our service) does not use a credit. If a credit was used for a reading that clearly failed (for example, no moves were read from a clear photo), tell us and we will give the credit back.

Not refundable

  • Free credits.
  • Credits already used for readings.

How to ask

Reply to the receipt email from Paddle, find your order at paddle.net, or email builder@hintwise.org with your order number. Refunds go back to the way you paid and can take a few business days to appear.

Deleting your account

Unused paid credits are lost when you delete your account, so ask for any refund first.
