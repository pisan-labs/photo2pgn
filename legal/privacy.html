Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers Photo to PGN at photo2pgn.hintwise.org, provided by HintWise. Questions, or requests to see or delete your data: builder@hintwise.org.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: your email address and the basic profile your sign-in provider shares (such as your name), kept by HintWise sign-in in Supabase (United States). It is one account for every HintWise app.
  • Your games: what was read, your corrections and notes, and the PGN. They are kept in your browser. When you are signed in they are also kept in your HintWise account in Supabase (United States), where only you can see them.
  • A record of each reading with credits: when it happened, the model, its size in tokens, its cost and whether it worked. It is used to count credits and limits. It does not contain your photos or moves.
  • Purchases: Paddle handles your payment details; we never see your card number. Paddle tells us what you bought (your email address, the pack, the amount and your country for tax).

Your photos

Your photos are used only to read the game. They go from your browser to Anthropic, which runs Claude:

  • With your own API key, straight from your browser to Anthropic.
  • With credits, through the HintWise gateway, which passes them on without keeping them.

Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

In your browser

Your browser keeps your sign-in session, your games, your settings and, only if you tick "Remember on this device", your Anthropic API key. Your key is sent only to Anthropic. We use no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who else handles your data

  • Anthropic: reads the photos.
  • Supabase: accounts, saved games, and the records of readings and credits.
  • Netlify: hosts the site, and keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and running the service.
  • Paddle: sells credits, and handles payment and tax.
  • Lichess: only if you press "Open in Lichess". Games imported there are public on Lichess.
  • Chess TD: only when Chess TD opened Photo to PGN to read a game and you press "Send to Chess TD". The game goes to the Chess TD page that opened it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete a game from My games. Delete your account from the account panel (click your name at the top right): this deletes your account and your data in every HintWise app, saved games included. Records of purchases are kept, without your account, for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. Email builder@hintwise.org. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Children under 13 should use Photo to PGN with a parent, guardian or coach. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data without consent, tell us and we will delete it.

Where your data is

Supabase keeps your account and saved games in the United States. Anthropic, Netlify and Paddle may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own terms.

Changes

We may change this policy. The date at the top says when it last changed.
