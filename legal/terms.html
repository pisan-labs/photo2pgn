1. What Photo to PGN does

It reads photos of chess scoresheets with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, checks every move with a chess engine, and gives you the game as a PGN file. Handwriting is often unclear, and the engine may fill in or change moves so that the game is legal. Readings can be wrong. Check the moves before you rely on a game, for example for ratings, prizes or appeals.
