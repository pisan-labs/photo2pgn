# Briefing

- Purpose: a web app that turns a photo of a handwritten chess tournament scoresheet into a downloadable PGN file. A HintWise app, deployed on Netlify.
- Scope (2026-11-12): upload 1 to 2 photos; Claude (Opus 5.5, structured output, adaptive thinking, effort high) reads moves and header; a deterministic engine (chess.js + beam search) checks legality and proposes repairs; the person reviews and edits; PGN download or copy. A demo game runs the review with no API call.
- Who pays (2026-11-12, owner, after the Plabs platform hand-off): free readings use the owner's key only through the HintWise LLM gateway, for signed-in users, within the gateway's caps (photo2pgn's own caps are set by the owner in the platform). A visitor's own key goes from the browser straight to api.anthropic.com. No Anthropic key on Netlify or in the app; no own rate limiter for the owner's key.
- Key decisions:
  - The reading runs in the browser through the platform template's `ask()`. The gateway has no structured output or streaming, so Claude answers with one `record_scoresheet` tool call, checked with zod. Photos are shrunk to 1568 px and the request to under 1 MB.
  - Sign-in, account deletion and model calls are the platform template (`src/lib/{supabase,auth,account,llm,signin-styles}.ts`). The HintWise footer is embedded byte for byte with a sha256 test; HintWise favicon.
  - The CLI reader stays (owner yes, 12 Nov) as the one exception to "photos stay in the browser": for signed-in users, photos wait in Netlify Blobs until `cli/pgn-worker.mjs` reads them with local Claude Code on the subscription, then are deleted. Its readings are counted per person with Blobs counters (3 a day, 10 a week by default), since the gateway caps do not cover it. `VITE_FREE_READER` picks gateway, local, or local-then-gateway. Owner, 13 Nov: halflife reads everyone's sheets for now (local-then-gateway; the gateway only when no reader is running or the reader's ceiling is reached), and the page does not wait: it says to check back in 1-3 minutes and collects the finished reading into My games through `/api/my-jobs`. A sheet should cost one credit either way; the platform will not add a charge mode (Plabs, 13 Nov), so photo2pgn takes it with a one-token gateway call made as the person when a read sheet is first delivered (option A, on the branch; live only with the owner's yes, since it spends on the key).
  - Claude reads and the engine decides: the prompt asks for faithful transcription, `_` for unreadable characters and alternatives; the engine (beam search) picks the cheapest legal game. For awful handwriting it guesses any legal move with a natural-move prior and backtracks to earlier moves that could be to blame, even clearly written ones. Stopping costs 1 per unplayed ply; clustered fixes and fixes to clear readings cost extra, so garbage stops at one red move. A person's illegal edit stops there. Shifted sheets (a move skipped or written twice): a cell can be left out or a missing move inserted before it; PGN numbers come from the game.
  - Server settings are `PGN_` prefixed: `CLAUDE_EFFORT` and `ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL` from the dev shell once leaked into the app.
- Feature parity with Notamate (owner, 12 Nov): board editing (tap to move), flip, explicit insert/leave-out, editing past a red move, notes, Lichess one-click import (public API, games public there), Chess.com via copy + analysis page (no public import), email or share sheet, My games (device always; Supabase app_photo2pgn.games when signed in, migration 0002, no photos), phone-first capture with turn/crop, installable app with Android share target. Not done: UI in other languages; paid plans.
- Pending: Plabs applies the migrations (gateway registration) and raises photo2pgn's gateway max_tokens (4096 is too few for a long game with thinking); the owner imports the repo in Netlify and Plabs attaches photo2pgn.hintwise.org. Real photos of bad handwriting for testing: owner, later.
- Non-goals for now: storing games server-side, accounts beyond sign-in for caps, PDF or multi-game sheets, a manual move-entry mode.
- Ways in (owner, 12 Nov): "Sign in" or "Use my own API key", nothing presented as a membership and no storage details up front; the photo step opens only once one is set up; a signed-in person out of credits is offered only the own key until the reset (`src/lib/access.ts`, where buying credits would be one more option). The printable scoresheet is gone.
